About

I am a fourth-year Ph.D. candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at The Ohio State University, advised by Prof. Jia (Kevin) Liu and Prof. Yingbin Liang.

My research studies optimization methods for modern machine learning problems, especially when learning systems are distributed, bilevel, or multi-Objective.

I am actively seeking research and applied science internship opportunities for Summer 2027. I am also always happy to discuss research ideas and explore potential collaborations. Please feel free to reach out at qin.681@osu.edu.
