About Me

I am a fourth-year Ph.D. candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at The Ohio State University, advised by Prof. Jia (Kevin) Liu and Prof. Yingbin Liang.

My research focuses on machine learning optimization, including bilevel optimization, distributed and federated learning, matrix-aware optimization, and communication-efficient learning.

I am currently seeking research and applied science internship opportunities for Summer 2027.
