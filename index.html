About

I am a fourth-year Ph.D. candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at The Ohio State University, advised by Prof. Jia (Kevin) Liu and Prof. Yingbin Liang.

My research studies optimization methods for modern machine learning problems, especially when learning systems are distributed, bilevel, or matrix-structured.
